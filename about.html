About Project Evergreen

Project Evergreen is the official in-character digital hub for all online platforms associated with the Democratic States of Toin. It is designed to offer a cohesive, immersive gateway into Toin’s government, culture, history, and public services through a unified web interface.

Purpose

The site acts as a master directory and entry point for all Toinan websites. Whether you're exploring the workings of the National Police Service, reviewing legislation from the General Assembly, or reading cultural news from The Colonist, Project Evergreen brings everything together in one place.

In-Character Worldbuilding

This site is a work of worldbuilding. All pages linked through Project Evergreen represent fictional yet realistic institutions within the imagined nation of Toin. These sites follow the design language, cultural context, and governance structures established for the Toinan universe.

Why “Evergreen”?

The name reflects Toin’s Pacific Northwest aesthetic, commitment to continuity, and the evergreen nature of its institutions — always growing, always alive. It also captures the tone of a nation rooted in nature, law, and civic identity.

Credits

Project Evergreen was conceptualized and developed by Richard MacKenzie as part of an ongoing digital worldbuilding effort. All content, design, and structure are part of the fictional canon of Toin.
